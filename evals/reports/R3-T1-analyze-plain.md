# UI/UX audit: Ledgerly static web app (index, invoices, signup)

**Screens reviewed:** `index.html` (landing), `invoices.html` (signed-in invoice detail + list), `signup.html` (account creation), shared `styles.css`   **Evidence:** all code read / scan.py / screenshots at 1280px and 400px / contrast.py on 21 pairs. No live interaction (static files, all links are `#` stubs).
**Assumed top user tasks (inferred from the copy; nobody specified them):** 1) A newcomer understands what Ledgerly is and creates a free account. 2) A customer finds an invoice and acts on it (send reminder, mark as paid, download). 3) A customer creates and sends a new invoice.

## Verdict

Beyond tweaking in two places: the landing page never says what Ledgerly is until the last block and its only sign-up path is an unfocusable black `<span>`, and the signup form demands a card number, household income, date of birth, and address for a "free account" through 11 unnamed controls. The invoices screen is functional but has no screen name, no "you are here", five competing solid buttons with DELETE the loudest, and a "Mark as paid" button that looks disabled (1.68:1). There is no design system at all: 0 tokens, 0 media queries, 14 font sizes, 22 colors, 18 spacing values, and the layout breaks at 400px. Biggest strength: the underlying content is sound (a clear feature list, a sensible invoice table, a correctly quiet per-row Archive action), so most of the work is removal and ranking, not invention.

## Top fixes

1. **Rebuild the signup form around the task** - keep name, email, password; drop phone, address, DOB, occupation, income, and card; real `<label for>` on every control; accept any phone/card format if ever asked later; inline per-field errors instead of the permanent "Error: invalid input."; remove "Clear form"; label the button "Create account"; delete the instruction paragraph. Why: two of the four Criticals live here and this is the conversion step. Effort M. Resolves 1, 2, 37, 38, 39, 40, 57, 59.
2. **Make actions real and ranked** - turn "Create your free account" into a real `<a>`/button and make it the single solid primary on the landing page, with plain "Sign up" and "Log in" entries; on invoices make one primary (Send reminder), "Mark as paid" a legible secondary, Download/Duplicate tertiary, Delete quiet and set apart. Why: a top task is keyboard-unreachable and another looks disabled. Effort S. Resolves 3, 4, 18, 19, 30, 31.
3. **Rewrite the first screen by removing** - replace "Welcome to Ledgerly! / Work. Smarter. / synergy blurb" with a 6 to 8 word tagline and the existing "Send invoices in seconds" points; delete "About this section", the "Which one are you?" chooser, and most of the 8 promos; state the 3.5% fee next to pricing. Why: a newcomer cannot answer "what is this?" or "where do I start?". Effort M. Resolves 17, 20, 21, 22, 23, 26, 27.
4. **Fix navigation and wayfinding once, in the shared header** - brand top-left linking home, plain section names (Home, Invoices, ...) with Hive/Pulse/Toolbox flagged for the owner, a two-cue active state, 4 to 5 quiet utilities, an `<h1>` screen name that matches the link clicked and the `<title>`, a marked current item in the sidebar, small ">" breadcrumbs, plus `header/nav/main/footer` and a skip link. Effort M. Resolves 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 28, 29, 41.
5. **Introduce a minimal token set and a small-screen layout** - about 9 greys + primary + semantic shades that pass contrast, a 6 to 7 step type scale in px/rem at weight 400+, a spacing scale, one radius, one top-lit shadow scale, content max-widths, and media queries for the header, promo grid, button rows, and table. Effort L. Resolves 5, 6, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 43, 44, 45, 46, 47, 53.
